Guardian V1 · Initial benchmark

Guardian V1 began the investigation into prediction reliability for zero-shot video action recognition. The original benchmark code was developed in the early EdgeHAR-X workflow.

Motivation

High relative softmax scores are not necessarily calibrated probabilities of correctness. A monitoring baseline was needed before evaluating more elaborate probes.

Why V2 followed

The early prototype did not, by itself, provide a user-facing way to inspect activity decisions across an uploaded video. V2 explored that interface and temporal reporting problem.

Limitations

Version-specific measurements, reproducible startup commands, and source files have not been verified for this publication. No V3 results are attributed to V1.
