Guardian: from prototype to controlled evaluation

Guardian examines whether additional observations of a frozen video model help distinguish correct predictions from errors. This site records the engineering progression, measured findings, and unresolved limitations.

Why further research was needed

Building a monitor and displaying its outputs does not establish that it reliably detects errors. V3 introduced controlled comparisons. Later TemporalGuard and HMDB51 studies investigated whether any signal survives dataset shift.

Research methodology and limitations

Static documentation only. This site does not run X-CLIP or provide a live inference service. V1/V2 implementations and V3 training scripts are excluded from the reduced repository.
