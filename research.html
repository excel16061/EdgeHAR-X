Research methods and subsequent studies

TemporalGuard and dataset shift

Phase 2B evaluated frozen monitors on HMDB51 mapped categories, not all 51 classes. The primary set contained 797 clips in 12 categories; the secondary set contained 177 clips. The sets share source groups and are not independent replications.

Confirmatory outcome

None of the three preregistered primary hypotheses met the prespecified rejection criteria. H2 and H3 remained inconclusive, rather than demonstrating equivalence or no effect. Descriptive improvements did not pass all required corrections.

Engineering validation

The original P3 gradient check failed; a separately preregistered P3-v2 validation passed. The original failure remains recorded. No adversarial robustness result was established.

Adaptive Temporal Evidence

ATE proposes allocating additional temporal observations to uncertain clips. Its Study E real-data evaluation has not been executed. Synthetic software tests are not evidence of improved accuracy.

Reproducibility

Aggregate results and analysis documents are available in the reduced repository. Video inference modules, some per-clip records, and full rerun capability are withheld. Independent replication has not occurred.
