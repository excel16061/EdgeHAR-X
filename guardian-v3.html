Guardian V3 · Controlled evaluation

Frozen Guardian-O and Guardian-Full monitors were evaluated against maximum softmax probability (MSP) on 505 source-group-disjoint UCF101 clips across 101 candidate classes. Clean top-1 accuracy was 68.7%.

Measured results

Risk difference versus MSP: Guardian-O −0.7 percentage points (95% interval −2.5 to +0.9); Guardian-Full −0.8 points (−3.3 to +1.7). Neither difference was statistically significant.

Why the work continued

The additional inference cost did not establish a reliable benefit. Subsequent research tested temporal consistency and the behavior of monitoring signals under cross-dataset shift.

Limitations

Evaluation used the official UCF101 training partition with group separation, because the official test list had been inspected earlier. Results are internally verified, not externally replicated. The reduced repository omits training scripts and per-video records.
